## ∫ CALCULUS — `LIMITS → DERIVATIVES → INTEGRALS`

The foundation of higher mathematics. Calculus is where you learn to think about change, accumulation, and approximation.

- **Spivak, "Calculus"** — The gold standard for rigorous single-variable calculus. Proof-based, challenging, and rewarding. Not for the faint of heart, but if you want to *understand* calculus rather than just compute, this is the book.
- **Stewart, "Calculus"** — The standard university textbook. Clear explanations, abundant exercises, and strong coverage of applications. Good for a first pass or for reference.
- **MIT OCW 18.01SC** — Single Variable Calculus, with video lectures, problem sets, and exams. Free and complete.
- **3Blue1Brown, "Essence of Calculus"** — A visual, intuitive series that builds geometric intuition for derivatives, integrals, and the Fundamental Theorem. Watch this *before* or *alongside* a formal course.
- **Khan Academy** — Free practice problems and explanations for limits, derivatives, integrals, and series.

`LIMITS → DERIVATIVES → INTEGRALS`

## 🧮 LINEAR ALGEBRA — `VECTORS → MATRICES → EIGEN`

Linear algebra is the language of higher mathematics. It underpins everything from differential equations to machine learning.

- **Strang, "Introduction to Linear Algebra"** — The classic MIT textbook. Clear, geometric, and applications-oriented. Pairs with Strang's MIT OCW lectures.
- **MIT OCW 18.06** — Linear Algebra, with Gilbert Strang's full video lectures. Free and complete.
- **3Blue1Brown, "Essence of Linear Algebra"** — A visual series that builds intuition for vectors, matrices, determinants, and eigenvalues. Essential viewing before a formal course.
- **Axler, "Linear Algebra Done Right"** — A proof-based, determinant-free approach for students who want rigor. Best after a first course.
- **Khan Academy** — Vectors, matrices, and transformations with practice problems.

`VECTORS → MATRICES → EIGEN`

## 📈 DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS — `ODES → PDES → SOLUTIONS`

Differential equations model change in the real world—physics, engineering, biology, and economics all depend on them.

- **Boyce & DiPrima, "Elementary Differential Equations"** — The standard undergraduate text. Clear, comprehensive, and well-organized.
- **MIT OCW 18.03** — Differential Equations, with video lectures and problem sets. Free and complete.
- **Arnold, "Ordinary Differential Equations"** — A rigorous, geometric treatment for advanced students.
- **3Blue1Brown, "Differential Equations"** — A visual series that builds intuition for ODEs and PDEs.

`ODES → PDES → SOLUTIONS`

## 🔍 REAL ANALYSIS — `RIGOR → PROOFS → FOUNDATIONS`

Real analysis is where calculus becomes rigorous. It's the bridge between computation and proof.

- **Rudin, "Principles of Mathematical Analysis"** — Known as "Baby Rudin." Terse, elegant, and demanding. The standard text for a first rigorous analysis course.
- **Abbott, "Understanding Analysis"** — A gentler, more explanatory introduction to analysis. Excellent for self-study.
- **MIT OCW 18.100A** — Real Analysis, with video lectures and problem sets. Free and complete.
- **Tao, "Analysis I"** — A clear, modern, and rigorous introduction by a Fields Medalist.

`RIGOR → PROOFS → FOUNDATIONS`
